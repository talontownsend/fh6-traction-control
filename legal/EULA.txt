1. WHAT THE SOFTWARE DOES

The Software reads telemetry that Forza Horizon 6 publishes over a local
network port, and creates a virtual game controller that mirrors your physical
controller with the throttle and brake reduced to limit wheel slip. It does
not modify the game, its files, or its memory, and it does not read the game's
memory. It only ever reduces your throttle and brake inputs; it cannot apply
inputs you did not make.
